# 🗂️ Guides & Courses

Here you can find [[🗂️ Guides|various guides, written or in video form]] how to accomplish various tasks. There are guides every interest

If you want more guided introductions on how to work with Obsidian, you can also check out the various [[🗂️ Courses]] offered by Community members.

Are you rather looking for examples to get inspired? Then you might want to check out [[🗂️ Showcases & Templates|the examples and showcases]] instead. 

Do you already have a good idea of your workflow, but are missing the tools to implement it? Browse plugins and themes on [community.obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md/plugins) or via the in-app community plugin/theme browser instead. This Hub no longer maintains its own directory of them.
