## Features

- [[Themes with Friendly Settings|Friendly settings]]: Supports the [[obsidian-style-settings|Style Settings]] plugin

## Customization Options (Style Settings Plugin) 
- Dark Mode Surface Contrast: Choose whether the editor or side panels feel slightly darker.
- Light Mode Gold Intensity: Keep the warmer gold pop, or soften it for a more restrained paper look.
- Header Accent Style: Keep the H4 gold break or run a full emerald cascade.
- Link Emphasis: Increase how much links stand out, especially in light mode.
- Dark Mode Text Warmth: Soften dark-mode text from cool white to warm parchment.
