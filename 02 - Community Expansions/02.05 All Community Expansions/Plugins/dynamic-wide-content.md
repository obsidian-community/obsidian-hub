# Dynamic Wide Content

Plugin ID: `dynamic-wide-content`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/pcoletsos/obsidian-dynamic-wide-content) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=dynamic-wide-content)
