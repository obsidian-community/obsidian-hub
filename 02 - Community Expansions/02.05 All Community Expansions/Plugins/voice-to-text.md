# Voice to Text

Plugin ID: `voice-to-text`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/satosprod/voice-to-text) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=voice-to-text)
Developed by: [[satosprod]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Push-to-talk voice transcription using Deepgram or Groq. Hold a hotkey to record, release to transcribe and insert at cursor.
