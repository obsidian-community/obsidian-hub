# ArtinOrs Advanced Kanban

Plugin ID: `artinors-kanban`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/artin0r/artinors-kanban) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=artinors-kanban)
Developed by: [[artin0r]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

A powerful kanban board. Markdown-backed boards with drag & drop, due dates, tags, WIP limits, checklists, note links, archive, search/filter and multiple boards.
