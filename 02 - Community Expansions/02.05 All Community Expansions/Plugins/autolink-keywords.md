# Linkosaurus

Plugin ID: `autolink-keywords`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/polygonhunter/linkosaurus) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=autolink-keywords)
