# Ledger Governance

Plugin ID: `ledger-governance`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jsdosanj/ledger) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=ledger-governance)
Developed by: [[jsdosanj]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Document governance for your vault: a status lifecycle (draft to published), owner and review tracking, a governance dashboard, and an in-vault audit log. 100% local - no account, no network.
