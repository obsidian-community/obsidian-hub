# Mathify Notes

Plugin ID: `mathify-notes`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/katananananna/obsidian-mathify-notes) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=mathify-notes)
Developed by: [[katananananna]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Intuitively formats chemical formulas (H2O, Ca(OH)2, ions), nested fractions, powers, variables, and math symbols as you type.
