# Graph Scroll Pan

Plugin ID: `graph-scroll-pan`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/junghyunbak/graph-scroll-pan) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=graph-scroll-pan)
Developed by: [[junghyunbak]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Pan the graph view by scrolling instead of zooming. Zoom with pinch or Cmd/Ctrl+scroll. Great for trackpad users.
