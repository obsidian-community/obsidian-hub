# LaTeX Symbol Picker

Plugin ID: `latex-symbol-picker`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ryxwaer/obsidian-latex-symbol-picker) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=latex-symbol-picker)
Developed by: [[ryxwaer]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Draw or search a LaTeX symbol in a side panel and insert it at the cursor, auto-wrapping in math delimiters when needed. Powered by a Detexify-style handwriting classifier.
