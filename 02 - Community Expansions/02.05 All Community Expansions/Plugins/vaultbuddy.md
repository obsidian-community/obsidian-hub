# VaultBuddy

Plugin ID: `vaultbuddy`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/zhenghaoyang24/obsidian-plugin-vaultbuddy) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=vaultbuddy)
Developed by: [[zhenghaoyang24]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

AI-powered chat assistant that stays in the sidebar. Supports streaming responses, chats with your entire vault as context, and provides answers grounded in your notes. Compatible with OpenAI-style APIs (DeepSeek, OpenAI, etc.).
