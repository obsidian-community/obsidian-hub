# Quick Date Picker

Plugin ID: `quick-date-picker`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ritalee2333/obsidian-quick-date-picker) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=quick-date-picker)
Developed by: [[ritalee2333]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Quickly summon a mini calendar by typing @ (or your custom trigger) in documents to pick dates and choose output formats on the fly. Also supports relative date shortcuts like @+3d.
