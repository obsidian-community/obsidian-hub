# Diacritics-Free Search

Plugin ID: `diacritics-free-search`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/spenhos/obsidian-diacritics-free-search) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=diacritics-free-search)
Developed by: [[spenhos]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Search and replace ignoring diacritics — Hebrew nikud, Arabic tashkil, Latin accents, and more. Works in active note and vault-wide.
