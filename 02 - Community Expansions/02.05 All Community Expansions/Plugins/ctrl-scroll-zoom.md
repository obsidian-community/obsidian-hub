# Ctrl+Scroll Zoom

Plugin ID: `ctrl-scroll-zoom`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/hata-suriiken/obsidian-ctrl-scroll-zoom) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=ctrl-scroll-zoom)
Developed by: [[hata-suriiken]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Zoom the whole app in and out with Ctrl + mouse wheel (scroll up to zoom in, down to zoom out), like a browser. Trackpad pinch-to-zoom works too.
