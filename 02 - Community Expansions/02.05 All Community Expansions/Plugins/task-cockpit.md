# Tasks Cockpit

Plugin ID: `task-cockpit`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/devidcode/obsidian-tasks-cockpit) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=task-cockpit)
Developed by: [[devidcode]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

A single, hand-orderable list of your vault tasks grouped by due date. Auto-aggregated, drag to reorder (order persists), auto-refreshed.
