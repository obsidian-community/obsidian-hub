# Photo Search

Plugin ID: `photo-date-search`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/appendjeff/photo-search-obsidian-plugin) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=photo-date-search)
Developed by: [[appendjeff]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Jump from daily notes to your photos on Google Photos or Immich. Uses dates from your daily note to format the photo URL search.
