# Themed Journal Capture

Plugin ID: `themed-journal-capture`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/sharfaroz7/themed-journal-capture) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=themed-journal-capture)
