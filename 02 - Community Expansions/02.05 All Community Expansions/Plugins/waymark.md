# Waymark

Plugin ID: `waymark`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/walktalkmeditate/pilgrim-obsidian) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=waymark)
Developed by: [[walktalkmeditate]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Import your Pilgrim walks (.pilgrim) as linkable Markdown notes — your transcribed voice reflections become searchable, linkable text, with stats, waypoints, weather, and an optional route map.
