# Life in Weeks

Plugin ID: `lifeweeks`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/micha12323/lifeweeks-obsidian) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=lifeweeks)
Developed by: [[micha12323]]

Render a life-in-weeks grid over your vault's weekly and daily notes. Click a cell to open its note, long-press for the day view.
