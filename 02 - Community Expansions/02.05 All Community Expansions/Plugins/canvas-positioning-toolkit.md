# Canvas Positioning Toolkit

Plugin ID: `canvas-positioning-toolkit`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/valleytheknight/Canvas-Positioning-Toolkit) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=canvas-positioning-toolkit)
Developed by: [[valleytheknight]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Auto-layout, arrange, and move Canvas cards without precise dragging: grid-tidy a messy board, pick up and place a card with a hotkey, or find and grab a card by text search in a dense cluster.
