# Agentic Chat

Plugin ID: `agentic-chat`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/tardigrde/obsidian-agentic-chat) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=agentic-chat)
