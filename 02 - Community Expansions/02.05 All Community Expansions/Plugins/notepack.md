# NotePack

Plugin ID: `notepack`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/kynatro/notepack-obsidian) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=notepack)
Developed by: [[kynatro]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Scans your vault's markdown files for unchecked todos and collates them in a todos sidebar. Supports @mention for team members and natural language due date logic.
