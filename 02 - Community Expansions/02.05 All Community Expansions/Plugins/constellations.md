# Constellations

Plugin ID: `constellations`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/nikshukrem/obsidian-constellations) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=constellations)
Developed by: [[nikshukrem]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

A 3D star map alternative to Graph View: notes are stars, shared tags form constellations, top-level folders form universes.
