# Quick Chinese Translate

Plugin ID: `quick-zh`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/sany2ng0226-boop/quick-zh) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=quick-zh)
Developed by: [[sany2ng0226-boop]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

One-click translate the whole current note into Simplified Chinese as a new copy. Title and filename are translated too, frontmatter is preserved, and code blocks are kept untouched. Supports Google, DeepL, and OpenAI-compatible LLMs.
