# Finance Tracker

Plugin ID: `finance-tracker`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/arangutambo/finance-tracker) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=finance-tracker)
Developed by: [[arangutambo]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Log spending as tags in your daily notes, then turn them into pace-aware budget, recurring-bill, split-expense, trip, forecast, net-worth, and savings dashboards. Capture via Apple Shortcuts or a quick-add modal.
