# Scroll Wheel Zoom

Plugin ID: `scroll-wheel-zoom`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jwczju/scroll-wheel-zoom) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=scroll-wheel-zoom)
