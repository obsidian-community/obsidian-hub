# JotDrop

Plugin ID: `jotdrop`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/diexar-labs/jotdrop) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=jotdrop)
Developed by: [[diexar-labs]]
