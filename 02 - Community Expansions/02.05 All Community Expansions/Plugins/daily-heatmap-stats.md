# Daily Writing Stats

Plugin ID: `daily-heatmap-stats`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/xsixteen/obsidian-daily-heatmap) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=daily-heatmap-stats)
