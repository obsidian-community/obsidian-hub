# Zotero Cite Links

Plugin ID: `zotero-cite-links`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jpeacock29/zotero-cite-links) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=zotero-cite-links)
Developed by: [[jpeacock29]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Cmd-click a Pandoc @citekey in your notes to open the cited PDF in Zotero at the page locator from the citation.
