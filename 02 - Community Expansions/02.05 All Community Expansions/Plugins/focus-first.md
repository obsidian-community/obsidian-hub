# Focus First

Plugin ID: `focus-first`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/christian-luger-at/obsidian-focus-first) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=focus-first)
Developed by: [[christian-luger-at]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Automatically sorts your existing tasks into an Eisenhower or Value/Effort matrix by due date, priority, and size. Pin a task when context beats the rule, with one click back to automatic.
