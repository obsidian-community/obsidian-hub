# Jira Sync Projects Issues Dashboard

Plugin ID: `jira-sync-dashboard`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/nabheetcloud/Obsidian-Jira-Sync-Projects-Issues-Dashboard) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=jira-sync-dashboard)
Developed by: [[nabheetcloud]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Sync one or many Jira projects into your vault as notes, and view a local, filterable KPI dashboard (projects, sprints, assignees, statuses).
