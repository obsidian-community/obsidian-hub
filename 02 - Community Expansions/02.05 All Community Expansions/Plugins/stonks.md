# Stonks

Plugin ID: `stonks`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/dmgx0/obsidian-stonks) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=stonks)
Developed by: [[dmgx0]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Show live stock, ETF, and crypto prices inline as you type a ticker. Do light math on quotes to track your own portfolio right in your notes. Fetches from Yahoo Finance and works on mobile.
