# CAD Viewer

Plugin ID: `cad-viewer`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/liamzy2021/Obsidian-CAD-DWG-DXF-viewer) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=cad-viewer)
Developed by: [[liamzy2021]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Preview and render DXF / DWG CAD drawings directly inside your notes (based on mlightcad/cad-viewer with Three.js WebGL). Supports both formats with full rendering.
