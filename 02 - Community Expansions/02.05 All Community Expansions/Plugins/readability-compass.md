# Readability Compass

Plugin ID: `readability-compass`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/maxonamission/obsidian-readability-compass) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=readability-compass)
Developed by: [[maxonamission]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Know the reading level of your notes and whether it fits your readers: one language-independent score (LIX), a CEFR-style target band you choose, always visible in the status bar, with a detail panel and commands.
