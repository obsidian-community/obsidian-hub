# Encrypted Datasafe Notes

Plugin ID: `encrypted-datasafe-notes`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/xauravww/obsidian-encrypted-datasafe-notes) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=encrypted-datasafe-notes)
