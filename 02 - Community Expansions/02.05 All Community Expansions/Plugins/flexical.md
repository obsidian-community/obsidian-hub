# FlexiCal

Plugin ID: `flexical`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/kev-cao/obsidian-flexical) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=flexical)
Developed by: [[kev-cao]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

A flexible calendar plugin to display the notes you want to see in a calendar view. You pick the filter and color, and FlexiCal will do the rest.
