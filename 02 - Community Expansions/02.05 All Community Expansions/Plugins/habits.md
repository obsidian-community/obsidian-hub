# Habits

Plugin ID: `habits`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jamescliffordspratt/obsidian-habits) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=habits)
Developed by: [[jamescliffordspratt]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Habit tracker with streaks, charts, and statistics. Log daily habits from a carousel dashboard or sidebar panel, pause habits without losing streaks, and export printable PDF reports.
