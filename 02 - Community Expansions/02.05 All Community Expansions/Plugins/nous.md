# Nous

Plugin ID: `nous`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/andymdh/obsidian-nous) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=nous)
Developed by: [[andymdh]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Turns meeting recordings, voice memos, photos, and PDFs into a tagged, linked knowledge graph - auto-summarized, cross-linked, and organized into wiki pages. Works with Claude, GPT, Gemini, GLM, a local model, or your Claude subscription.
