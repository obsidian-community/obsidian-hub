# Slide and Reveal

Plugin ID: `slide-and-reveal`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/grub-basket/SnR) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=slide-and-reveal)
Developed by: [[grub-basket]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Scroll-to-reveal flashcard study for diagrams and labeled images. Right-click a folder to open it: drop covers over each label, then scroll in place to uncover them one pair at a time. Per-folder, no slide duplication required.
