# Character Companion

Plugin ID: `character-companion`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/devilsurvivor2/character-companion) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=character-companion)
Developed by: [[devilsurvivor2]]

Shows animated character sprites living in the side panel and walking along the bottom edge, with click animations, speech bubbles, and an optional reactive comment feed.
