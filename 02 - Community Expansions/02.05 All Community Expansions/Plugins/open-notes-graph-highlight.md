# Open Notes Graph Highlight

Plugin ID: `open-notes-graph-highlight`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/4cjyxbq25cb/obsidian-open-notes-highlight) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=open-notes-graph-highlight)
Developed by: [[4cjyxbq25cb]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Highlights currently open notes in the graph view — custom color, enlarged size, and dimming of other nodes. Includes an in-graph control panel.
