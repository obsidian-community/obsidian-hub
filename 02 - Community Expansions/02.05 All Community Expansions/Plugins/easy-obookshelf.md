# Easy Bookshelf

Plugin ID: `easy-obookshelf`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/cocoaai-it/easy-obookshelf) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=easy-obookshelf)
Developed by: [[cocoaai-it]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Manage your bookshelf from ISBN. Fetches book metadata from NDL, Google Books, and Open Library, then creates and organizes book notes.
