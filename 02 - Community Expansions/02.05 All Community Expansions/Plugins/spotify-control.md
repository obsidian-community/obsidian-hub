# Spotify Control

Plugin ID: `spotify-control`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/caezium/obsidian-spotify-control) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=spotify-control)
Developed by: [[caezium]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Control Spotify from your notes. Now-playing sidebar with hover controls, time-synced lyrics via LRCLIB, queue, search, transport hotkeys, and insert-track-into-note. Display works on Free; playback control requires Premium.
