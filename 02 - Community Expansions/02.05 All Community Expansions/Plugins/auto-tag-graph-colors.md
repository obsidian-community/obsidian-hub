# Auto Tag Graph Colors

Plugin ID: `auto-tag-graph-colors`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/trisko06/auto-tag-graph-colors) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=auto-tag-graph-colors)
Developed by: [[trisko06]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Automatically assign distinct, stable colors to every tag in the graph view. Includes smart tag blending, monochrome mode, and heat coloring by connection count.
