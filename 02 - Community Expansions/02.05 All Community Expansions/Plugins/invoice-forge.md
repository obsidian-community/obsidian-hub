# Invoice Forge

Plugin ID: `invoice-forge`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/israerusan/obsidian-invoice-forge) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=invoice-forge)
Developed by: [[israerusan]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Find the billable work hidden in your notes and turn it into an invoice. Tag work #billable, generate numbered invoices, and mark each billed so nothing is missed or billed twice. Pro adds PDF export, tax, multi-currency, and due-date reminders.
