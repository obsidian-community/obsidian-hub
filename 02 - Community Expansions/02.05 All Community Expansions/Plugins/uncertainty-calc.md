# Uncertainty Calculator

Plugin ID: `uncertainty-calc`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/masoudmim/obsidian-uncertainty-calc) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=uncertainty-calc)
Developed by: [[masoudmim]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Spreadsheet-style calculator that propagates measurement uncertainty: input forms, significant figures, expanded uncertainty, uncertainty budgets, and Monte Carlo, following the GUM and its Supplement 1.
