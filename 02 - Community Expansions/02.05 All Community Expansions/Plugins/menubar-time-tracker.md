# Menu-Bar Time Tracker

Plugin ID: `menubar-time-tracker`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/powderfirm-ship-it/obsidian-menubar-time-tracker) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=menubar-time-tracker)
