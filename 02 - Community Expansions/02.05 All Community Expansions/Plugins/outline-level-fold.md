# Outline Level Fold

Plugin ID: `outline-level-fold`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/josipmarkus/obsidian-outline-level-fold) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=outline-level-fold)
Developed by: [[josipmarkus]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Fold and unfold the active note's outline to a chosen depth (levels 1-6) with hotkey-bindable commands.
