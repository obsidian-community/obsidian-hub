# Advanced Wikipedia Importer

Plugin ID: `advanced-wikipedia-importer`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/dyllonwright/Wikipedia-To-Obsidian-Markdown) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=advanced-wikipedia-importer)
