# lilbee

Plugin ID: `lilbee`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/tobocop2/obsidian-lilbee) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=lilbee)
Developed by: [[tobocop2]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

A private, self-hosted NotebookLM alternative for your vault: a local AI search engine and native model manager. One-click local models, cited answers (local RAG), and a web crawler. Works with Ollama, LM Studio, and frontier models too.
