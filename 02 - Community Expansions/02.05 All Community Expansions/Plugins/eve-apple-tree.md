# Eve Apple Tree Thinking System

Plugin ID: `eve-apple-tree`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/brandthebrand/eve-apple-tree) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=eve-apple-tree)
Developed by: [[brandthebrand]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Grow your notes into a living 3D tree of light - a thinking tool, not a filing cabinet. Each folder becomes a tree; notes are leaves placed by field and time; links are the rhizome; flowers are ah-ha moments; apples are outputs.
