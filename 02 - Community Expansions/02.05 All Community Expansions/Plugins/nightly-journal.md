# Nightly Journal

Plugin ID: `nightly-journal`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/damofer/nightly-journal) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=nightly-journal)
Developed by: [[damofer]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

A local AI interviews you at the end of your day and writes your journal for you: daily notes, people and projects, a confirmable writing plan, and a long-term memory you can ask questions. 100% local via Ollama, in English or Spanish.
