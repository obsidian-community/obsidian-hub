# Spherical Knowledge Graph

Plugin ID: `spherical-knowledge-graph`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ironmind15/spherical-knowledge-graph) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=spherical-knowledge-graph)
