# JokeBakery Sync

Plugin ID: `jokebakery-sync`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/jimhooker/jokebakery-obsidian) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=jokebakery-sync)
Developed by: [[jimhooker]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Two-way sync of your JokeBakery jokes, sprints, lists and crashes with your vault as Markdown. Requires a JokeBakery account.
