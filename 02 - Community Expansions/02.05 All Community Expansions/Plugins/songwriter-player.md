# Songwriter Player

Plugin ID: `songwriter-player`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/mrrepac/obsidian-songwriter-player) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=songwriter-player)
Developed by: [[mrrepac]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Advanced audio player for songwriting: waveform, work marker, A-B loop zone, play counters and hotkeys — in the sidebar, or replacing the plain player inline in your notes. Playback survives note scrolling and closing the panel.
