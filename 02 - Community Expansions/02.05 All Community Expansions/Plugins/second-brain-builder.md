# Second Brain Builder

Plugin ID: `second-brain-builder`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/algometrix/second_brain_builder) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=second-brain-builder)
