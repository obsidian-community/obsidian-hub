# Torah Verse Inserter

Plugin ID: `torah-verse-inserter`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/spenhos/obsidian-torah-verse-inserter) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=torah-verse-inserter)
Developed by: [[spenhos]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Insert Hebrew Bible (Torah/Tanakh) verses with nikud and cantillation, fully offline. Search Hebrew naturally with your regular keyboard — type 'bereshit' to find בראשית, no keyboard switching. Optional translations and AlHaTorah links.
