# Vimium+

Plugin ID: `vimium-plus`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/stianlyng/obsidian-vimium-plus) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=vimium-plus)
Developed by: [[stianlyng]]
Mobile compatible: [[Desktop-only plugins|No]]

Navigate with Vimium-style keys: f for click hints, j/k/d/u scrolling, J/K tab switching, and i to drop into the editor's native Vim mode.
