# Auto Date on Open

Plugin ID: `auto-date-on-open`
Links: [GitHub repository](https://github.com/kaicuichen-git/Obsidian-date-on-open) or [<button id=HH>Open in Obsidian</button>](obsidian://show-plugin?id=auto-date-on-open)
Developed by: [[kaicuichen-git]]
Mobile compatible: [[Mobile-compatible plugins|Yes]]

Adds today's date to a note when you open it, using your own line template, date format and choice of which notes to include.
